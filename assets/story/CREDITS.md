# Story art (Idle Restaurant Manager)

Everything here is AI-generated; the prompts are in `tools/genart.mjs`.

These were generated on 2026-11-09 with OpenAI's image API (model `gpt-image-2.6-flare`, quality medium) by
`tools/genart.mjs`:
- `letter`, `diner`, `scooter`, `smash`: the story cards.
- `stage2`…`stage5`: the restaurant's stages, each an edit of the one before (`diner` is stage 1), so it stays one building.
- `cook-btn`: the COOK button.
- `away`: the "while you were away" card.

These were generated on 2026-11-10 with GPT Image 2.6 through Higgsfield (model `gpt_image_2_6`, quality medium, 1k), from the
same prompts:
- `dish-*`: the eight dishes, cut from one sheet (`dishes`).
- `veh-*`: the couriers' five motors, cut from one sheet (`vehicles`).
- `ferdi`: Uncle Ferdi's portrait, cut out of its white background.

The webp copies, and the cutting, were made by `tools/genart-webp.py`.
